## 📌 Project Overview

Understanding customer behavior and identifying conversion opportunities are essential for successful marketing campaigns.

**Marketing Funnel & Conversion Performance Analysis** is a data analytics project focused on evaluating the performance of bank marketing campaigns using the Bank Marketing dataset.

The project explores customer conversion patterns across job categories, communication channels, campaign contact frequency, and age groups.

An interactive **Tableau Dashboard** was developed to transform raw marketing data into meaningful visual insights and support data-driven business decisions.

> **Project Goal:** Identify conversion patterns, understand campaign effectiveness, and recommend strategies to improve marketing performance.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall marketing funnel performance.
- Measure successful subscriptions and conversion rates.
- Identify customer segments with better conversion potential.
- Compare conversion performance across contact methods.
- Understand the impact of campaign contact frequency.
- Explore age-wise conversion behavior.
- Develop actionable recommendations for marketing optimization.

## 📂 Dataset Information

**Dataset:** Bank Marketing Dataset

**Source:** [Kaggle – Bank Marketing Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/adityamhaske/bank-marketing-dataset)

The dataset contains customer information and details of direct marketing campaigns conducted by a bank to promote term deposit subscriptions.

### Key Dataset Attributes

| Attribute | Description |
|---|---|
| `age` | Age of the customer |
| `job` | Customer's occupation |
| `contact` | Communication method |
| `campaign` | Number of contacts during the current campaign |
| `duration` | Duration of the last contact |
| `previous` | Number of contacts before the current campaign |
| `poutcome` | Outcome of the previous campaign |
| `y` | Final subscription outcome |

**Target Variable – `y`**

- `yes` – Customer subscribed to a term deposit.
- `no` – Customer did not subscribe.

### Conversion Rate Formula

```text
Conversion Rate =
(Successful Subscriptions / Total Contacts) × 100
```
